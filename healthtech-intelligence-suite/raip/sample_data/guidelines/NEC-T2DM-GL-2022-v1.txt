CLINICAL MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
First-line pharmacologic therapy for adults with type 2 diabetes is a sulfonylurea.
Lifestyle counseling should accompany pharmacologic therapy.
This version does not discuss metformin as preferred initial therapy.

===PAGE 3===

MONITORING
Reassess glycemic control after therapy initiation.
Screen for hypoglycemia when using sulfonylurea therapy.
