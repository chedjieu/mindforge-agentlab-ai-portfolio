# endocrinology_ada_t2dm.md

## Glycemic targets

For most non-pregnant adults with type 2 diabetes, an A1C goal of <7% is reasonable
if achievable without significant hypoglycemia. Individualize for comorbidities and
life expectancy.

## Metformin and CKD

Metformin remains first-line for type 2 diabetes when eGFR >= 30. Reduce dose when
eGFR is 30-45 and discontinue if eGFR falls below 30. Reassess dosing at eGFR 58
(CKD stage 3a) - full dose may still be acceptable with GI tolerability monitoring.

## Intensification

When A1C remains above goal on metformin, add an agent with proven CV/CKD benefit
such as SGLT2 inhibitor or GLP-1 receptor agonist. Prefer oral pathways when patients
decline injectables; oral SGLT2i is an evidence-based alternative.
