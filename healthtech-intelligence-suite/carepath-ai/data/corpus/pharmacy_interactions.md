# pharmacy_interactions.md

## Common chronic-care interaction themes

ACE inhibitors with potassium supplements raise hyperkalemia risk — monitor serum K+.
Statins with strong CYP inhibitors may increase myopathy risk; atorvastatin dose
limits apply with certain interacting drugs. Low-dose aspirin for secondary prevention
is generally compatible with ACE inhibitors.

## Sedative combinations

Benzodiazepines combined with opioids (including tramadol) substantially increase
risk of respiratory depression and overdose — avoid concurrent use when possible and
counsel on naloxone where appropriate.
