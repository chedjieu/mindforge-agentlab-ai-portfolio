# Medical, Dental, Vision

Full-time eligible associates may enroll in medical, dental, and vision plans during open enrollment or qualifying life events.

# Retirement, Life, HSA

401(k) retirement, life insurance, and Health Savings Account (HSA) eligibility depend on role, tenure, and benefits plan assignment linked to the associate's role in the knowledge graph.
