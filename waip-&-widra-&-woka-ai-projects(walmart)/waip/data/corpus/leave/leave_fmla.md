# PTO and Sick Time

Paid Time Off (PTO) accrues by tenure and role. Sick balances may be separate by state. Associates can view balances in the leave system.

# FMLA and Medical Leave

Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) eligibility depends on tenure and hours worked. Medical leave may be paid or unpaid depending on available balances and leave type.

Maternity, parental, and military leave follow dedicated policy sections. State-specific overlays (for example California) may grant additional protections.
