# Payroll Discrepancy

A payroll discrepancy occurs when net or gross pay does not match expected regular, overtime, bonus, or leave coding for the pay period.

Common causes include missing hours, incorrect overtime, tax deduction changes, direct deposit updates, and unpaid leave hours excluded from regular pay.

# Medical Leave and Pay

Approved unpaid medical leave hours are excluded from regular wages for the affected pay period. If leave was approved but coded incorrectly, Payroll Ops can correct via a ServiceNow payroll ticket after review.

W-2 and year-end adjustments follow the annual payroll calendar. Associates should verify leave codes in Workday before contacting Payroll.
