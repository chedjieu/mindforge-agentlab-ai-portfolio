# Attendance Policy

Associates are expected to report scheduled shifts on time. Approved leaves (PTO, medical leave, FMLA) must be coded in the time system before payroll close.

Unexcused absences may result in coaching under the attendance policy. Pharmacy and store roles follow the same national attendance framework with state overlays where required.

# Ethics and Coaching

Walmart maintains an open-door ethics process. Coaching conversations are documented in HR systems. Disciplinary procedures escalate from verbal coaching to written coaching according to policy version latest.
