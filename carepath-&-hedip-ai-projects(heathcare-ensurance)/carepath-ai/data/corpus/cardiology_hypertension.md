# cardiology_hypertension.md

## Blood pressure goals

Target BP under 130/80 mmHg for most adults with diabetes and hypertension when tolerated.
ACE inhibitors or ARBs are preferred first-line with diabetes and albuminuria/CKD.

## Combination therapy

Many patients require ACEI/ARB plus calcium-channel blocker and/or thiazide-like
diuretic. Monitor potassium and creatinine after ACEI initiation or dose changes.
