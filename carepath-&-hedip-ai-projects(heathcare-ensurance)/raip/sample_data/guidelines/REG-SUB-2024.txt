REGULATORY SUBSTANTIATION GUIDANCE
Document ID: REG-SUB-2024
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Publisher: Synthetic Health Products Authority
Authority: Regulatory / Government Authority (Tier 1)

===PAGE 1===

PURPOSE
Promotional and clinical claims in regulated documents must be substantiated by cited evidence.
Unsupported medical or regulatory statements are a high-severity failure.

===PAGE 2===

REQUIREMENTS
Do not invent clinical recommendations.
Do not cite a generic source.
Do not fabricate a citation.
If evidence is insufficient, the authoring system must state that additional authoritative evidence is required.
Outdated guidance that has been superseded must not be presented as current.

DISCLOSURES
